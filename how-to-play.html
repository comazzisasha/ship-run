How to Play Ship Run

Ship Run is a free Geometry Dash online fan game all about GD ship mode. It runs in your browser on a PC, Mac, Chromebook, phone or tablet, with nothing to download and no login.

PLAY SHIP RUN

Geometry Dash ship mode controls

Space, Up arrow, mouse or finger
Hold to fly up. Let go to fall.
Esc
Pause the game.

The ship tilts toward where it is going. Landing on a block or the floor is safe, but flying into the side of a block, a spike, a saw or a thorn pit ends the attempt.

Game modes: the levels and the random mix

  • Play opens the levels, in order from easiest to hardest. Only the first one is open at the start: beat a level to unlock the next. Crashing restarts that level, and your best % is saved.
  • Random Mix unlocks once you have beaten every level: 5 levels picked at random and played back to back. Crash once and a new mix starts with new levels.

Portals, pads, orbs and user coins

  • Speed portals change how fast you move: half, normal, double, triple and quad speed.
  • Gravity portals flip gravity upside down, or put it back to normal.
  • Size portals make the ship mini (smaller and quicker to react) or big again.
  • Teleport portals move you straight to their exit.
  • Pads launch the ship as soon as you touch them.
  • Orbs launch the ship when you press while touching them.
  • User coins count once you touch them and then reach the end of their level without crashing.

Some spikes fade away as you get close, and some blocks are invisible, so watch out.

The level editor: make your own Geometry Dash levels online

The editor is part of dev mode, which is locked. Once dev mode is unlocked, press Tab to open the dev menu and pick Level Editor. The editor works like the Geometry Dash editor:

  • Create, Edit and Delete tabs, swipe selection, rotate, flip, free move and snap.
  • Editor layers: switch layers with the pink arrows, go back to all layers with the blue arrow, and click the layer number to lock a layer.
  • Edit Group: editor layers, Z order, Z layers, group IDs and extra options like Hide and No Touch.
  • Edit Object: color channels, HSV and copying colors between objects.
  • Color and alpha triggers, a test button that plays from the start or from where you are, and saving your levels.

Play on PC, Chromebook, Mac or mobile

Ship Run is an HTML5 browser game. It works in Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari on Windows PCs, Macs, Chromebooks, iPhones, iPads and Android devices, with no app, extension, download or account needed.

Games like Geometry Dash

If you like rhythm platformers such as Geometry Dash, Geometry Dash Lite, Meltdown, World or SubZero, Ship Run gives you a free, ship-mode-only take on the same idea: one button, music-timed obstacles and instant retries. Work through the levels one by one, then take on the Random Mix.

Settings

Choose Geometry Dash art or your own pictures, the graphics quality, Low Detail Mode, and whether the progress bar, percentage, attempt counter and level name are shown.
